Classroom-use terms

This project is a restricted CSCI 599 classroom demonstration, operated by Karthik Venugopal.

Permitted use

Access is limited to the operator and authorized graders. Use dedicated test accounts and fictional email, calendar, preference and project data. Do not connect personal or USC mailboxes or use the prototype to contact real third parties.

Human decisions

Review generated suggestions before relying on them. Calendar suggestions do not modify events. An email can be sent only after an authenticated human reviews and approves its exact recipient, subject and body. Do not use the project for consequential real-world decisions.

Availability and limitations

Responses can contain errors. Availability depends on cloud services, provider quotas and network connectivity. Local inference additionally depends on the operator’s laptop and Ollama. Private requests are held when local inference is unavailable; ordinary non-private requests can use cloud.

Data handling and access

Use of the assistant involves the processing described in the privacy and data-use notice. Access credentials and machine-share invitations are provided privately and should not be posted publicly. The operator may withdraw access when assessment and regrades are complete.
